SUPPORT THE TOUR

Build the Next Generation of Democracy Defenders


The Election Defense Movement School Tour is bringing together a thousand young people across the country to build a bold new generation of organizers in the fight for free, fair elections and a just democracy for all.

Your support helps make every tour stop possible, from training materials and meals to travel, speakers, and Election Defense Toolkits that participants take home with them.


$15

Help provide meals, refreshments, and supplies for one tour stop so every participant can be well cared for while they spend the day learning, organizing, and building community with one another.


$25

Help provide one participant with a Movement School Election Defense Toolkit, including election defense buttons, stickers, and know your rights resources they can bring home to educate and mobilize folks in their own community.


$75

Help Generation Vote national team and partner organization trainers facilitate know your rights, election protection, post-election canvass monitoring and curing, and creative action planning trainings and workshops for one movement school tour stop.


$100

Help the Generation Vote team post-tour as they follow up with participants, coordinate with state Election Protection coalitions, and connect tour attendees with poll monitoring shifts, post-election canvass monitoring and ballot curing efforts, and other opportunities to defend democracy in their own communities.


$150

Help fund photography and videography at each tour stop so Generation Vote can create recruitment videos, social media content, and educational resources that inspire more young people to get involved in election defense work and our movement to fight for our right to vote and a just democracy for all.

Our mission: To spark an intersectional youth-led movement that fights for our right to vote and a just democracy for all.


Generation Vote Education Fund supports our movement’s work to educate the public about electoral justice issues and youth voting rights, provide robust educational training programs and leadership-development support to our GenVote Teams/Hubs, spearhead national nonpartisan youth election protection, and build partnerships with organizations to address our generation’s democratic crisis.

Please note that Generation Vote Education Fund is currently fiscally sponsored by The Social Good Fund, an independent 501(c)(3) organization. Donations intended for Generation Vote Education Fund are processed through Social Good Fund and then re-granted to Generation Vote Education Fund through a Model-C partnership. Gifts made to a 501(c)(3) nonprofit are tax-deductible.
