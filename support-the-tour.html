Build the Next Generation of Democracy Defenders


The Election Defense Movement School Tour is bringing together a thousand young people across the country to build a bold new generation of organizers in the fight for free, fair elections and a just democracy for all.

Your support helps make every tour stop possible, from training materials and meals to travel, speakers, and Election Defense Toolkits that participants take home with them.

Our mission: To spark an intersectional youth-led movement that fights for our right to vote and a just democracy for all.


Generation Vote Education Fund supports our movement’s work to educate the public about electoral justice issues and youth voting rights, provide robust educational training programs and leadership-development support to our GenVote Teams/Hubs, spearhead national nonpartisan youth election protection, and build partnerships with organizations to address our generation’s democratic crisis.

Please note that Generation Vote Education Fund is currently fiscally sponsored by The Social Good Fund, an independent 501(c)(3) organization. Donations intended for Generation Vote Education Fund are processed through Social Good Fund and then re-granted to Generation Vote Education Fund through a Model-C partnership.

Gifts made to a 501(c)(3) nonprofit are tax-deductible.

To spark an intersectional youth-led movement that fights for our right to vote and a just democracy for all.
The two organizations that support our movement are Generation Vote Education Fund, a 501(c)(3) fiscally-sponsored organization of the Social Good Fund and Generation Vote Action, a 501 (c)(4) political and advocacy nonprofit.

Generation Vote Education Fund supports our movement’s work to educate the public about electoral justice issues and youth voting rights, provide robust educational training programs and leadership-development support to our GenVote Teams/Hubs, spearhead national nonpartisan youth election protection, and build partnerships with organizations to address our generation’s democratic crisis.

Please note that Generation Vote Education Fund is currently fiscally sponsored by the Social Good Fund, an independent 501(c)(3) organization. Donations intended for Generation Vote Education Fund are processed through the Social Good Fund and then re-granted to Generation Vote Education Fund.

Generation Vote Action drives campaigns across the Generation Vote movement to educate young people about voting, community organizing, and mobilizing skills in order to achieve legislative and electoral victories that stop youth voter suppression, increase participation of young people in the democratic process and promote democracy reforms in the United States on the local, state and federal level. GenVote Action is organized to support our movement’s communications, leadership development programs, lobbying efforts and candidate endorsements (consistent with limitations on its activities as a 510c4 organization).

Gifts made to a 501(c)(3) nonprofit are tax-deductible, whereas 501(c)(4) gifts are not.
