BUILDING THE NEXT

GENERATION

OF ELECTION DEFENDERS

It’s no secret: the White House and their billionaire-backed allies are escalating their attempts to steal the 2026 elections and seize more power for themselves.


But we won’t let them decide our future or define what democracy looks like for our communities.

From August to September, GenVote and our allies are kicking off our national election protection program with the first national youth-led Election Defense Movement School. From schools, places of faith, rural counties, and cities across the country, we’ll invite young people and community members of all ages to join the growing election defense movement, bringing a bold new generation of organizers into the fight for free, fair elections and a just democracy for all.
